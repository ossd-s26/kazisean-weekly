As the saying goes "Time flies when you're having fun". I love open source projects and getting into [homelab](https://hossain.cc/posts/homelab/) made me appreciate open source projects even more. Looking back this class has been very fun in forcing myself to contribute to open source. <!--more--> 

Contributing to open source can be very daunting as at first from the outside it looks like everyone knows what they are doing. But working on [oppia](https://github.com/oppia/oppia/) past few months have made thought me a lot of lessons. One of the crucial lessons I have taken away from this class and this project in general is that if you stick enough long time even when something is hard, pogress is possible. I have also learned that infinite time means "never getting it done". Having the weekly meeting even sometimes meeting 2 or 3 times a week with my group with a strict deadline on what to finish before the meeting really helped push me to continue working on this project. Working on oppia has been a huge accomplishment, joy and also pain. Both pain and please this project brought to all of us in the group as at the start everything looked very promising and welcoming, in the middle felt unreliable and abandoned trying to understand the codebase and issues, but in the end submitting a pr to make a fix feels amazing. It is one of those feeling not describable through words, a breadth of euphoric feeling. So now that I am writing this and looking back maybe all of those struggles of debugging environment issues, digging through outdated docs and close to giving up made this moment even more sweet. Almost all of group memebers by now have published a pr or close to almost making one. This week we focused mostly on finish up our presentation for tomorrow and practice presenting with the group.

From the class presentation we took notes on what we liked. Most of the presentations were really cool. I really liked how all most all of the teams have broken down their challenges and how different teams have came up with different ways of getting attention / issue merged. Making personal connection with a maintainer is something I will be taking away with me. I also liked the props of red hats that were used during one of the presentation and overall was very proud of that group for picking a complex project and getting a really good feature merged. I also loved seeing some of the tools that I use and seeing my classmates contribute to them made me feel very proud. Overall I enjoyed all of the presentations as each were unique in the own way and none of them were boring in any sense.
